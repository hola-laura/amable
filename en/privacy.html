Enquiry privacy

This page explains how the data you send through the amable form is used.

Data controller

Laura Sampedro de la Rosa. You can get in touch and exercise your rights by writing to lrsmpdr@gmail.com.

For what purpose and on what basis

I use your business name, sector, what you need to improve and the contact details you leave to reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, to prepare information about a possible service. I don't use this data for marketing or profiling. The legal basis is taking the pre-contractual steps you request when you write.

How the data is received

The form sends the data to a Google Apps Script project linked to this website. The enquiry is saved in Google Sheets and I get a notification in Gmail. Google provides these services and may process the data under its privacy and processing terms.

n8n is not involved in receiving these enquiries. I don't sell or share your data with other businesses. I would only disclose it if required by law.

How long

I will keep your enquiry while I deal with it and for up to six months after our last contact. If a rule requires some data to be kept longer, or it is needed for a claim, I will keep only what is necessary for that period.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection to processing, and request portability where applicable, by writing to lrsmpdr@gmail.com. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

What happens if you don't provide the data

Your business name, what you want to improve and a way to contact you are needed so I can reply. If you'd rather not provide them, you can write to me directly by WhatsApp or by email.
