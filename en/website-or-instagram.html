Do you need a website if your business already has Instagram?

Instagram helps people discover your work. A website gives you a stable place to explain, organise and convert interest.

What Instagram does well

Instagram supports visual discovery, updates and conversation. Its reach and features are controlled by the platform, and not every potential customer has an account.

What a website adds

A website can explain services, prices, location, hours and booking in one place. It can appear in search and link to your tools. You control its domain and structure, so information is easier to find and maintain.

Use both for different jobs

Start with a home page, service pages, contact details and a booking route. Link it from Instagram. Treat social media as discovery and your website as a reliable source of information and action.
