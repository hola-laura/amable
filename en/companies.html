Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know what to automate?

No. You tell me how your team works and we look at it together in the first conversation, which is free.

Do you work with companies outside Madrid?

Yes. I work remotely with companies all over Spain. If you're in Madrid, I can also come to your office.

Do we have to change the software we use?

No. What I build connects to what you already use: spreadsheets, email, calendar or your management software.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

The AI does on its own what it can do well, and anything important is always reviewed by someone on your team.

How much does it cost?

Custom projects start at €3,000 excluding VAT. Before we start I give you a fixed price with the scope and timeline.
