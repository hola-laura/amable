Local SEO: how to appear when nearby customers search

Accurate details, useful service pages and genuine local trust help customers understand where you work.

Complete your business profile

Claim and verify your Google Business Profile, choose accurate categories, and keep hours, contacts and photos current. Use consistent details in trusted directories. Avoid duplicate listings and misleading locations.

Write useful local pages

Explain each service, who it helps and where you deliver it. Use natural customer language and practical details. Avoid near-identical pages for every neighbourhood with only the place name changed.

Build trust over time

Invite honest reviews and earn relevant local mentions from real partners. Keep pages fast and easy to use on mobile, with clear contact actions. Review enquiries and search performance regularly.
