How to get customers online: a practical guide

Help the right people find you, trust you, get in touch and come back—without relying on one platform.

Map the customer journey

Discovery, understanding, trust, contact and return are separate steps. Ask recent customers how they found you and what nearly stopped them choosing you. Fix the weakest step before spending more on promotion.

Make it easy to choose you

Complete your Google Business Profile, keep details consistent and invite honest reviews. Give each service a clear page explaining who it is for, what is included, where you work and how to book. Track enquiries by source and follow up promptly.

Keep customers close

Send useful reminders and follow up after enquiries. Choose one or two channels you can maintain. A clear website, trustworthy profile and good service create a stronger base than trying to post everywhere.
