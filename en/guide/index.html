Questions

Before you download it

Is it really free?

Yes. You download it from this page without paying anything and without leaving your details.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. You use it by writing ordinary sentences. If you can send a message on your phone, you can follow the guide.

Do I have to pay for any tool?

Not to get started: everything works with the free account. Only the scheduled tasks in chapter 18 need a paid Claude plan, and the guide recommends waiting until the assistant saves you time before paying for it.

Which AI tool does it use?

Levels 1 and 2 work with any of the big four: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. Level 3, setting up an assistant that works on its own, is shown in Claude, step by step and with screenshots.

Can I share it?

Yes, and we'd be glad. Better to send the link to this page, so whoever downloads it always gets the latest version.

Is it up to date?

It was written in November 2026. Tools change quickly and a button may have a different name; the way of working it teaches doesn't change.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes: La IA, pero amable, the same guide in Spanish.
