How much does custom software cost—and when is it worth it?

Compare a tailored system with the cost of workarounds and existing tools.

What shapes the cost

Scope depends on users, workflows, integrations, migration, security and support. A small internal tool differs from a customer platform with payments and roles. Hosting and maintenance matter too.

Check existing tools first

Map where the current process fails. Configuration, a better form or a small automation may be enough. Custom software fits best when the workflow is distinctive and repeated friction is significant.

Reduce risk with a small release

Estimate time, errors and delays conservatively. Agree on discovery, a minimum version, ownership, support and an exit plan. Test the core workflow before expanding.
