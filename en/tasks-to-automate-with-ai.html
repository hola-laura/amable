What tasks can a business automate with AI?

AI helps most with repetitive, well-understood tasks when a person can check the result.

Good candidates

Try sorting enquiries, drafting routine replies, summarising notes, extracting details from forms or preparing first content drafts. Reminders and moving information between tools can also reduce manual work.

Keep judgement with people

Do not leave consequential decisions about customers, money, health or access to an unchecked system. Review accuracy and context. Do not enter confidential information unless the tool’s data handling is suitable.

Map, test and measure

Document the trigger, inputs, action, exceptions and responsible person. Keep human approval for customer-facing changes. Compare time and errors before and after, and make sure the process can be paused and corrected.
