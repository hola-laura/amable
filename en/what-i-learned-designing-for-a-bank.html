What designing for a bank teaches about better digital experiences

Complex financial products sharpen lessons that apply to any business: reduce uncertainty and make the next step clear.

Put clarity where decisions happen

People need to know what will happen before committing. In banking that may be a repayment; in a small business it may be a price, appointment or cancellation policy. Show important information at the decision point.

Design the whole journey

Customers move between discovery, comparison, action and follow-up. Mapping the journey reveals missing confirmations, unclear responsibilities and dead ends after a form.

Use evidence and shared decisions

Data shows where people leave; research helps explain why. Test focused changes and agree on success before launch. Make assumptions and trade-offs visible so design, engineering and the business can improve together.
