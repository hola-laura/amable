What we are always asked

Can I switch accountants halfway through the year?

Yes. We request the paperwork from your previous accountant ourselves and start in the next quarter.

Do I have to come to the office?

No need. Almost everything is done by email and phone. If you’d rather come in, book a meeting.

What does the fee include?

All the regular returns and your questions. Anything outside it, we tell you in writing before we start.

What if I get a notice from the tax office?

We answer it. If the mistake was ours, it costs you nothing.
