About us

Fourteen years behind the same bar.

We opened in 2011 with a simple idea: a place near home for a properly poured vermouth, without going into the centre.

We still buy at the market next door and fry the torreznos to order. What has changed is that you now need to book on Saturdays.

Marta and Quique
