Questions we always get

Can the quote go up?

No. What we sign is what you pay. It only changes if you ask for something new, and we send that in writing before doing it.

Do I have to move out?

For kitchens and bathrooms, no. For a full renovation it is better to, and we tell you from the start how many weeks.

Do you handle the permit?

Yes. We file the works notice or building permit with the council and speak to the building’s management.

How do I pay?

In three parts: at the start, halfway through and on handover. By bank transfer.
