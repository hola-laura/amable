How it works

Four steps. You talk about your business; the kit does the rest.

You do it with Claude, an assistant you talk to in a chat. The kit teaches it to work the way a designer would.

01 · 20 MIN

The interview

Twelve questions about your business, each with an example answer. You can answer briefly, with typos or by dictating.

02 · 20 MIN

The brand

It shows you two styles with your colours and your fonts. You just say “A” or “B”.

03 · 30 MIN

The words

It writes your website in the words you used in the interview, thinking about how someone who doesn’t know you would look for you on Google. You point out what doesn’t sound like you and it gets changed.

04 · 30 MIN

The website

It builds your page on a finished design, made to look good on a phone and optimised for Google. You ask for changes and take it with you.

You can do it all in a day or one step each evening. At the end of each step you get a sheet with everything you’ve done, so you can pick up where you left off.

What you’re probably thinking

“I know nothing about computers.”

You don’t need to. If you can write a WhatsApp message, you can use the kit. It’s all questions and answers, and the installation guide takes you step by step, with pictures of what to click.

“Anything an AI writes sounds like a robot.”

It sounds like a robot when you ask it to make things up. Here it interviews you first, and the copy comes from your answers and your words. It also has a list of banned expressions: no “solutions tailored to you”.

“I don’t have good photos.”

The website looks good without them and leaves the spaces marked for when you do. The kit doesn’t invent photos of your premises or your team: the ones that appear will be yours.

“I don’t have a whole day.”

It’s four steps of 20 to 30 minutes. Do one each evening, after closing.

“I already have Instagram, that’s enough.”

To be seen, yes. To be found on Google, show your opening hours and get a message in one tap, you need a website. The two help each other.

“It can’t look good for that price.”

It looks good because the design is already done. What you pay an agency for is the hours spent deciding colours, fonts, order and copy. Those decisions are already made; you bring your business.

Frequently asked questions

Can it really be done in a day?

It’s about two hours of work spread over four steps. With the free version of Claude you may need to pause between steps; even so, it’s done in two or three sittings.

What is Claude? Do I have to pay for it?

It’s an artificial intelligence assistant you talk to in a chat, from your phone or computer. It has a free version and the kit works with it.

Will my website show up on Google?

It comes prepared for it: the title, the copy, the photos and your business details are optimised for search engines (SEO), with the searches for your area that you choose. The kit also gives you the steps for your Google Business Profile, which is what counts most for a local business. What nobody can promise you is first place: a new website takes a few weeks to appear.

Is the website mine?

Yes. You take your website file with you and can publish it wherever you like. You don’t depend on amable to have it.

Can I change it later?

Yes. You open a conversation, attach your sheet and ask for the change: a new price, different opening hours, a photo.

Does it work for an online shop?

No. The kit makes a one-page website that presents your business. If you need to sell online or a custom booking system, write to us and we’ll look at it separately.

What about the legal notice and privacy policy?

The website leaves the place ready, but those texts carry your company details and you or your accountant need to complete them before publishing.

What if I get stuck?

You fill in amable’s contact form, tell us which step you’re on and we reply by email.
