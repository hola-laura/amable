How much does business process automation cost? Set-up, tools and upkeep

“How much does automation cost?” has three answers, because you pay for it in three parts: building it, the tools each month, and keeping it running. Here’s the breakdown with real 2026 prices.

In short
  • Automation is paid for in three parts: set-up (once), tools (monthly) and upkeep (now and then).
  • At amable, a simple one-task automation costs €350 excluding VAT; company projects with several processes start at €3,000.
  • The tools have free plans to start with: Zapier gives you 100 tasks a month and Make 1,000 credits. Paid plans start at around $10–20 a month.
  • The sum that matters: hours saved per month times what your hour is worth, minus what you pay each month.

When someone asks us how much automation costs, they’re almost always thinking of a single figure. But there are three, and the one that surprises people isn’t the first: it’s the one paid every month without noticing, or the one that turns up when something stops working.

How much does it cost to automate a process?

It depends on the size of the process, but it always breaks down like this:

Set-up

This is the work of understanding how you do things today, deciding which steps to automate, building it, testing it with odd cases and explaining it to you. For reference, these are amable’s prices, excluding VAT:

One task€350A simple automation from start to finish: saving every enquiry and alerting you, or confirming and reminding appointments.
Booking€600Online booking with automatic confirmation and a reminder the day before.
Companyfrom €3,000Several connected processes: invoices, orders or documents passed around by hand today.

If someone gives you a price without asking how you work, be wary: they’re either overcharging or going to fall short.

Monthly tools

Platforms like Zapier or Make are often used to connect apps to each other. Both have free plans, and for a small business that’s sometimes enough:

One thing to watch: in Zapier, every step that does something counts as a task. A four-step automation that runs 50 times a month is 200 tasks, not 50.

Upkeep

An automation isn’t furniture: apps change, someone adds a field to the form, you switch calendars. A simple automation usually runs for months untouched, but you should know three things in advance:

  • Who’s told if it fails. Platforms send an email when something doesn’t run; that email needs to reach someone who reads it.
  • What a tweak costs. Ask before you start, not once it’s broken.
  • Whose name the accounts are in. The tools should be in your name, not the name of whoever builds it.

Are there grants for automation?

In Spain, the Kit Digital, the state programme of digitalisation grants for small businesses and the self-employed, closed for applications in 2025, according to the government’s November 2025 review. If you’re looking for grants now, check with your Chamber of Commerce or regional government. In the UK, look at what your local growth hub offers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to automate a process in a small business?

It depends on the size. At amable, a simple one-task automation costs €350 excluding VAT and company projects with several processes start at €3,000. On top of that are the monthly tools, which are sometimes free.

How much does Zapier cost per month?

There’s a free plan with 100 tasks a month. The Professional plan starts at $19.99 a month billed annually ($29.99 monthly), with 750 tasks, according to its pricing page in autumn 2026.

Is Make cheaper than Zapier?

For similar volumes, Make is usually cheaper: its free plan gives 1,000 credits a month and the Core plan is around $10 a month. But what matters most is how many steps your automation has and how often it runs.

Are there still Kit Digital grants for automation in Spain?

Applications for the Kit Digital closed in 2025. Today it’s worth checking calls from your Chamber of Commerce or regional government.
